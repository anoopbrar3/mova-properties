What the data partner brings

The aircraft connection

Onboard acquisition from the aircraft data buses, analysis in the air, and transmission of alerts and key parameters to the ground while the aircraft is still flying. This is hard, certified engineering, and it is not what AeroBee does.

What AeroBee brings

The decision inside the window

The alert becomes a cited response plan: probable cause ranked against this tail's own history, the applicable manual procedure, the technician qualification needed, tooling, and candidate parts at the arrival station.

Why it needs both

Neither half works alone

Telemetry without an intelligence layer is a notification. An intelligence layer without live telemetry is a post-flight report. Together they convert flight time into preparation time — which is the only place the money is.

About this scenario. Simulated for illustration using a representative A320 hydraulic event, with timings drawn from typical line-maintenance practice rather than a specific operator. Both lanes assume the same alert, aircraft, crew and station. Real figures depend on your fleet, your stations and your data latency, and would be measured during a pilot rather than asserted here. AeroBee is advisory software: it locates evidence and flags where it's missing, and every recommendation shown would be reviewed by authorised personnel who retain full decision authority.
