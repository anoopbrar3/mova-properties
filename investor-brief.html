Behind the multifamily pipeline sits a track record of 41 homes delivered, including four luxury estate residences and nine single-family homes across BC and Alberta — the standard that shapes every rental building that follows.

Strategy

  • Build-to-hold. We create value through development, then keep the asset to capture cash flow, amortisation, and appreciation over a 10+ year hold.
  • One platform, four disciplines. MOVA acquires, designs, builds, and operates — no handoff, no agency gap between builder and owner.
  • Design-led. Distinctive, durable buildings command rent premiums, lower vacancy, and retain residents — compounding NOI and value.
  • Supply-constrained BC markets. Greater Victoria, Langford, and Greater Vancouver, where vacancy runs consistently below 2%.

Returns & Financing

  • CMHC MLI Select. Insured financing with high loan-to-cost and amortisations up to 50 years — reducing equity and rate exposure.
  • Yield-on-cost. Stabilised targets in the 5–7% range, with equity appreciation layered through design and operational discipline.
  • Multiple return engines. Development margin, refinance, cash flow, amortisation, and appreciation — not a single exit.
  • Conservative underwriting. Every project stress-tested against lower rents, higher costs, and longer timelines.
